# Projekt J.E.N.O.V.A Community Wiki Guidelines

Author : [TheAenema](https://github.com/TheAenema)

---

### Welcome to the Jenova Community Wiki

Here’s what you need (or don’t need) to follow when submitting a community article:

1. **No specific writing format is required. You are free to share your knowledge however you want.**

2. **Add your article to the `/pages` directory** using the following filename format:
   
   ```
   <Author>@<ArticleName>.md
   ```
   
   *Example:* `TheAenema@BuildingOnLinux.md`

3. **Start your article with the following template:**

   ```markdown
   # Article Full Title

   Author: [<AuthorName>](https://github.com/<AuthorID>)

   ---
   ```

4. **Add your article’s path to `Pages.txt`** so it can be indexed by the search system.

   *Example:*

   ```
   Welcome.md
   /wiki/pages/TheAenema@BuildingOnLinux.md
   ```

5. **Add your article to `Welcome.md`** under the Wiki Database section.

   *Example:*
   ```markdown
   ## Wiki Database
   
   - [Building Jenova Runtime On Linux](/wiki/pages/TheAenema@BuildingOnLinux.md)
   ```

You are free to format everything else however you like.

> **Tip:** You can use GPT or GitHub Copilot to help format or edit your article quickly.
